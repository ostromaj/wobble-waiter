# Wobble Waiter

The chef won't stop throwing stuff onto your tray. Slide left and right to keep the tower balanced — pancakes, a cat, a fishbowl, a bowling ball, a live chicken — while dogs, toddlers, banana peels and the kitchen door try to knock it all over.

- Runs last 5–45 seconds; one tap to try again
- Daily Special: same items and chaos for everyone each day
- Challenge links: share `#beat-12` and your friend sees the number to beat
- Phone (drag) or computer (mouse / arrow keys)
- No ads, no purchases, no chat, no accounts — saves stay on the device

Play: https://ostromaj.github.io/wobble-waiter/
